A little more English with every story.

English Tales helps Turkish-speaking learners practise English through short stories. Explore 50 stories at A1, A2 and B1 levels, see Turkish sentence translations and save useful words for later practice.

DISCOVER FAMILIAR STORIES
Read short, level-adapted versions of Cinderella, Rapunzel, Snow White, The Snow Queen and more. Discover stories from Aesop, the Brothers Grimm, Hans Christian Andersen, Oscar Wilde and O. Henry, alongside original English Tales stories.

READ, UNDERSTAND AND LISTEN
• Tap sentences to see Turkish translations.
• Explore word meanings and build your saved vocabulary.
• Listen using your device's text-to-speech support.
• Adjust text size, line spacing and reading theme.
• Open downloaded stories, covers and vocabulary offline.

PRACTISE YOUR VOCABULARY
Review with flashcards, translation questions, word matching and letter-building activities. Follow your words through learning, reviewing and known stages.

BUILD A READING HABIT
Choose a daily goal, continue where you left off and track your learning activity. Turn on optional reminders. Meet your friendly book companion during onboarding and celebrate completed stories together.

START AT YOUR OWN PACE
Begin as a guest, or sign in with Google or email to sync your learning progress.
