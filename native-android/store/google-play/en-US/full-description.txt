Build your English reading habit with short stories at A1, A2 and B1 levels. Read a story, check sentence translations and return to useful words in your review plan.

READ AT YOUR LEVEL
Explore 50 adapted stories, including classic tales, fables, mysteries and everyday adventures. Choose a level and continue where you left off.

UNDERSTAND WHAT YOU READ
Tap sentences for translations in your support language. Tap highlighted learning words to see their meanings and save them for practice.

TURN NEW WORDS INTO PRACTICE
Review with word cards, translation questions, matching and spelling activities. Use story questions to check your understanding.

READ AND LISTEN
Listen with your device’s English text-to-speech engine. Adjust text size, line spacing and reading theme. Audio availability, including offline playback, depends on the voice data installed on your device.

TAKE STORIES WITH YOU
Download stories and covers to read offline. Downloads and saved words stay available on your device.

MAKE A SMALL DAILY HABIT
Choose a daily goal, follow your progress and set optional reminders. Start as a guest or sign in with Google or email to sync supported learning records.

Story translations are available in Turkish, Latin American Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Hindi, Russian, Bengali, Urdu and Thai. The interface is also available in English; English interface mode currently uses Turkish story translations.
