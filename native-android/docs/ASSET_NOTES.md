## Kullanılan son istem

Create a single production illustration asset for an English learning mobile app, transparent background. A cheerful friendly female reading guide, waist-up, short wavy violet hair, large round black glasses, warm peach skin, expressive smiling eyes, wearing a turquoise sweater, holding a small open yellow book; alongside her on the right a tall stack of colorful books with cyan, pink, yellow and purple covers. Polished flat 2D mobile learning app cartoon, clean smooth shapes, delicate shading, bold vibrant colors, friendly approachable proportions, similar production quality to a modern language learning app mascot. Entire character and book stack visible with comfortable transparent padding. Portrait composition. No text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark. This must be an original character.
